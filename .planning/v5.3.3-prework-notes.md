### Phase E — BACKLOG-02 (voseo) testing strategy

Snapshot/deterministic assertions will NOT catch model variance. Flag at planning time so the executor doesn't default to a normal regression test. Options:

1. **Multi-run sampling with statistical threshold** — N runs, assert voseo appears in ≥X out of N (e.g., "in N=20 runs, voseo appears in ≥18"). Costs N× model spend per CI run.
2. **Accept-list of acceptable forms** — list both "tenés" and "tienes" as PASS, only fail on neither appearing. Cheaper, weaker signal.

Decide at Phase E planning time based on CI budget and how strict the brand voice requirement is.
