<objective>
Protect Wellhub bookings from every cancel path that belongs to El Templo's own plan lifecycle (D-11 matrix in 204-RESEARCH.md): staff single remove (D-11) and member self-cancel (D-24) are rejected; plan cancel/pause/change (`cancelFutureBookings`), invitation void (`cancelUpcomingBookingsForMember`), compensate-days range cancel (`cancelBookingsInRange`), the branch-change bulk cancel in `updateMember` and the member soft-delete bulk cancel (D-25) skip Wellhub rows. Class closure and schedule deletion keep cancelling Wellhub bookings (out of scope: deferred `cancelBookingByGym` gap), but the credit grant of `cancelBookingsFromDateAndGrantCredits` excludes them (D-18: a Wellhub booking never grants replacement credits to the socio's plan).

Purpose: a Wellhub booking is managed in the Wellhub app; cancelling it on our side desyncs Wellhub and billing.
Output: guards in booking-service, booking-population, members service (branch change) and members DELETE route, integration tests.
</objective>
