## Context

This plan builds the first 4 content sections of the /academy page plus the page shell. It establishes the `academy-` BEM prefix, the centralized data file, and the page composition pattern. The remaining 6 sections are in plans 43-03 and 43-04.

**CONTEXT.md locked decisions:**

- Accordion: 7 modules, single-open, Geologica Semibold 16px header, chevron rotates 90deg, max-height + opacity 0.3s ease
- Certification cards: Nivel 1 active (Terracotta border, hover), Niveles 2-3 at opacity 0.6 with badges, activable via flag
- SEO title: "Olympic Academy — Formaci&oacute;n de Entrenadores | El Templo Calistenia"
- /curso-entrenadores 301 redirect to /academy
- Remove /academy from nuxt.config.ts prerender ignore list

**Spec backgrounds:**

- Hero: Image + overlay Deep Charcoal (0.20 -> 0.60)
- QueEs: Marble Cream #F2EDE5
- Programa: Warm Stone #D9CFC1
- Niveles: Marble Cream #F2EDE5
