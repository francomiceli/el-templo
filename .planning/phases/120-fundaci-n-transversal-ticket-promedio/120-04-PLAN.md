<objective>
Deliver Block 6 (Ticket promedio) — the first real consumer of the Phase 120 foundation — as a new read-only domain service plus its route, response contract, and integration tests. The service computes, per currency: the per-plan ticket = mean of the LINKED `subscriptions.price_paid > 0` (TICKET-01), the global ticket = `SUM(price_paid) / COUNT(charges)` volume-weighted (TICKET-02, NOT a mean of plan means), the $0 charge count/% reported side-by-side (D-03), the average + median discount vs list price using the `price_regular_snapshot` (Plan 02) with a current-`priceRegular` fallback + disclaimer for historical rows (TICKET-03/D-05), a two-cohort split (list-price vs discounted/customized) so discounts/overrides do not silently distort the headline, and a mandatory `excludedNoLink` count for `plan_charge`s with no subscription link — all opened by duration/branch/country via the Plan 03 breakdowns engine (TICKET-04).

Purpose: Validate the foundation end-to-end and ship a correct, READABLE ticket metric the manager can trust. The charge UNIVERSE/PERIOD is the Phase 105 canonical revenue filter restricted to `kind='plan_charge'` ONLY (NOT debt_settlement), `direction='inflow'`, `voided_at IS NULL`, by `transaction_date`, respecting the panel `[from,to)` range, isolated per currency. But the per-charge ticket VALUE and the discount numerator come from the LINKED `subscriptions.price_paid` (the agreed membership price), NOT from `financial_transactions.amount` (cash received, which can be a partial payment and would falsely report partial payments as discounts).
Output: ticket-service.ts + types + schema + /ticket route + integration test.
