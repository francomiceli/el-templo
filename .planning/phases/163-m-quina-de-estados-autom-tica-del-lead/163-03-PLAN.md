<objective>
Wire the source-of-truth writes for the state machine across the three modules that mutate lead state: (1) reset Perdido → En seguimiento (source auto) when a lead re-books a trial via admin `bookTrial` or self-service `reserveTrialSelfService`; (2) stamp `lead_status_source='auto'` in the purchase hook `recomputeUserStatus`; (3) stamp `lead_status_source='manual'` in the manual PATCH `updateLead` and `'auto'` at lead creation.

Purpose: These writes are what make D-04's auto/manual distinction meaningful — the cron (163-02) reads `lead_status_source` to decide what it may overwrite, and the reset (AUTO-03) is what lets a re-booked lead re-enter the window. Legitimate automatisms (purchase, reset) stamp `auto` and are allowed to overwrite a manual Perdido; only the human PATCH stamps `manual`.

Output: Three edited service files (all small, targeted `.set()`/`.values()` additions respecting existing invariants) plus an integration test.
</objective>
