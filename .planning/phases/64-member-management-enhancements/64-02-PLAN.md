Business rules from CONTEXT.md:

- Upgrade (new priceRegular >= current priceRegular): Allowed mid-cycle. Prorated credit applied, net amount charged.
- Downgrade (new priceRegular < current priceRegular): Blocked mid-cycle. Show expiry date.
- Class-based: credit = (remainingClasses / totalBudget) \* pricePaid
- Unlimited: credit = (daysRemaining / durationDays) \* pricePaid
- Integer pesos, no decimals.
