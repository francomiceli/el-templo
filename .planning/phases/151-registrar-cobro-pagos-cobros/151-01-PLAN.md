<objective>
Backend for COBRO-04: a transfer/card charge from the Cobros PoS must carry a valid bank-account id, validated server-side (type=banco + active + currency-match), persisted as the pendiente row's imputation. Add a coach-reachable endpoint to list the selectable accounts.

Purpose: Move the imputation decision to the point of sale (better data at origin) without breaking the v5.3 server-derived invariant or the multibanco correction downstream.
Output: `bankAccountId` field on the 3 PoS body schemas, `GET /coach-load/bank-accounts`, two `cashRegisterService` methods, and integration tests.
</objective>
