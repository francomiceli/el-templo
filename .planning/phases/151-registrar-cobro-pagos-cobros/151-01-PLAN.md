<objective>
Backend for COBRO-04: a transfer/card charge from the Cobros PoS must carry a valid bank-account id, validated server-side (type=banco + active + currency-match), persisted as the charge row's imputation on ALL FOUR PoS code paths — including the two paths (`/pay-plan` renew, `/alta`) that delegate caja resolution to `subscriptionService` and therefore need an override field threaded through `AssignPlanInput`/`RenewSubscriptionInput`. Add a coach-reachable endpoint to list the selectable accounts.

Purpose: Move the imputation decision to the point of sale (better data at origin) without breaking the v5.3 server-derived invariant or the multibanco correction downstream. Renovación (most common PoS flow) and alta+plan (Phase 148 flagship) MUST carry the chosen account — not just settle/misc.
Output: `bankAccountId` field on the 3 PoS body schemas, `cashRegisterIdOverride` threaded through the subscriptions service, `GET /coach-load/bank-accounts`, two `cashRegisterService` methods, and integration tests covering all 4 paths.
</objective>
